The Nook mark is a rounded `accent` square holding two overlapping windows, the front one carrying Pip's eyes. Use `assets/Logos/nook-mark.svg` for app icons, the dock and favicons (64, 32 and 20px) and `nook-wordmark.svg` beside it for the About window, the installer and the website. Clear space around the mark is one quarter of its width. Never recolour the mark, outline it, or set it on a busy photo; on dark surfaces it stays `accent` with white windows. The wordmark text is the system display face at 700, letter-spacing -0.035em.
