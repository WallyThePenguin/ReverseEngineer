Pip is Nook's agent and mascot: one ice-blue rounded cube with two dot eyes and a tiny mouth, floating in a soft glow. That is the whole drawing. Nothing is added to it, ever: no limbs, outline, cheeks, gradient, hat or accessory. Use the files in `assets/Mascot` for static art and the inline SVG from this preview where it should move; never redraw Pip or change its colours (`pip-body`, `pip-eye`, `pip-glow`, `pip-glow-ink`).

Pip's personality is in how it moves, not in detail. Idle: it floats, breathes (a slow squash and stretch), blinks every few seconds and glances around. Listening: it leans in, eyes wide, mouth open, glow strong and pulsing. Thinking: it drifts side to side, eyes up, three dots rise. Happy (a task finished, an approval given): arc eyes, a hop with squash on landing, three sparks drift up. Asleep (quiet hours): eyes closed, a slow bob, the glow dims, two z's. All motion is transforms on named groups (`pip-body`, `pip-eyes`, `pip-glow`), so it stays cheap; under reduced motion Pip holds the pose. Sizes: 20 to 24px in rows and chips, `pip-size` (40px) in cards and the island, 96px in empty states and onboarding.

Voice: Pip speaks in short plain sentences in first person ("I drafted a reply. Want to send it?"), never in exclamation marks, never about itself as an AI.
